4.  **Specific Floor Heights:** While "Foundation to Roof Lvl" is mentioned, exact floor-to-floor heights, especially the plinth level, are not detailed, which can be useful for comprehensive foundation interaction. (Criteria 10)
5.  **Soil Improvement Details:** No information regarding any soil improvement methods utilized at the site, which could be critical depending on site conditions. (Criteria 16)
6.  **Detailed Plan of Ties:** While tie details are given in the column schedule, a separate, specific plan for tie arrangements would improve clarity for complex layouts, often helpful for site execution. (Criteria 18)
7.  **Calculated Percentage of Steel (Columns & Footings):** The notes do not explicitly state the calculated percentage of steel (e.g., `As/Ag` for columns, `Ast/bd` for footings). While bar sizes are given, these percentages should be verified against minimum (0.8% for columns, 0.12-0.15% for footings) and maximum (6% or 4% for columns) limits as per IS 456:2000. (Criteria 19)
8.  **Explicit Cross-Section Area Usage:** The notes do not explicitly state whether gross or effective cross-sectional areas are used for calculations of columns and footings/slabs. While generally understood, explicit mention clarifies design assumptions. (Criteria 20)
9.  **Maximum Steel Percentage in Columns:** The maximum allowable percentage of steel in columns (6% or 4% if lapped) as per IS 456:2000 Clause 26.5.3.1 is not stated in the design notes. (Criteria 22)
